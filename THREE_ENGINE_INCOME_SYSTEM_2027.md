# PLAN LIFE GRATEFUL — THREE-ENGINE INCOME PLAN (2027)

## Operating principle
Build at least three distinct income engines with different failure modes. Share research, production, tracking, and automation infrastructure, but do not let one platform or one offer control the whole business. A stream is not "active" until eligibility, links, conversion tracking, and payout path are verified. Revenue is not real until a sale/commission is confirmed; cash is not received until payout lands.

## Engine 1 — Affiliate commerce
**Goal:** earn commissions from products that fit the audience.
- Primary discovery routes: TikTok affiliate/creator commerce and YouTube affiliate/Shopping where eligible; other reputable affiliate programs only after terms are checked.
- First tasks: verify account eligibility; apply through official pages; record approval, approved products, commission, disclosure rules, payout threshold, and payout method.
- Content: original demonstrations, problem/solution explainers, comparisons, and honest reviews. Never make unsupported health claims or imply personal use that did not happen.
- Resilience: maintain more than one approved offer and, where allowed, more than one traffic platform. Never assume one program can replace another until approved.
- Metrics: eligible clicks, conversion rate, verified orders, reversals/returns, commission pending, payout eligible, payout received.

## Engine 2 — Owned digital product
**Goal:** sell a useful product we control, so all income does not depend on affiliate approval.
- Audit existing products before creating another. Verify checkout, delivery, seller verification, refund terms, customer support, and payout setup.
- Choose one clear product for one specific problem; improve the existing asset if it is useful rather than generating duplicates.
- Examples to evaluate from existing assets: practical creator/automation resources and Islamic learning materials. Choose based on real demand, quality, and audience fit—not assumptions.
- Build a simple path: useful free content/resource → honest product explanation → checkout → delivery → follow-up/help.
- Metrics: qualified visits, checkout starts, completed orders, refunds, net proceeds, payout status.

## Engine 3 — Original media and audience monetization
**Goal:** build an audience asset that can eventually earn through platform monetization, music streaming/licensing, sponsorships, or other rights-compliant opportunities.
- Focus on original Plan Life Grateful content and music; retain ownership/permissions and follow platform music and monetization rules.
- YouTube ad revenue and platform monetization are conditional on current eligibility and review; do not count them as current income before approval and payout.
- Publish a consistent set of useful, distinct videos. Reuse concepts across platforms only with platform-appropriate edits and rights.
- Use successful-channel research to study hooks, pacing, audience promise, titles, retention, and repeatable formats—never copy protected work or fabricate results.
- Metrics: published assets, watch time/retention, returning viewers, subscribers/followers, monetization eligibility, verified royalties/revenue, payouts.

## Shared infrastructure
- HP EliteBook: control station, research, asset preparation, code and local tests. Check free disk space before installations.
- Cloud runtime: scheduled jobs only after deployment, uptime, logs, secrets, backups, and restart behavior are tested.
- Automation: audit existing Zapier and n8n first. Select one primary engine for each job; avoid duplicate production flows.
- Source of truth: one existing task ledger/queue after inspecting current projects. No new duplicate dashboard unless justified.
- Pipeline: IDEA → VERIFIED → READY → APPROVED → PUBLISHED/DELIVERED → METRICS → REVIEW.
- Reliability: unique IDs, deduplication, limited retries, failure alerts, backups, pause switch, and a documented rollback.
- Security: no secrets in prompts/repositories/logs; use secure credential stores.
- Approval: human approval for public posts, spending, customer messages, account changes, or anything with financial/reputational risk.

## 30-day execution order
### Days 1–3: prove access and payout paths
1. Audit the EliteBook and current cloud/automation accounts.
2. Verify TikTok affiliate eligibility and YouTube affiliate/Shopping eligibility in official account interfaces.
3. Audit existing digital products, checkout, seller verification, and payout configuration.
4. Record blockers and evidence. Do not invent a fourth project.

### Days 4–7: launch the smallest income tests
1. Apply to eligible programs and track the actual application state.
2. Choose one compliant affiliate offer and produce three distinct original content tests.
3. Fix one existing digital product/checkout bottleneck, if present.
4. Publish original media consistently, with public posting still approval-gated.
5. Build one harmless cloud automation test before automating revenue-critical steps.

### Weeks 2–4: measure, improve, diversify
1. Compare results by platform, offer, and content format.
2. Keep, improve, or stop tests based on evidence—not views alone.
3. Add another offer or distribution channel only when it reduces dependency and can be maintained.
4. Verify all sales, refunds, commissions, fees, and payouts.
5. Produce a weekly continuity report: what fails if TikTok, YouTube, one affiliate program, a scheduler, or the cloud host becomes unavailable?

## Universal agent command
Audit the current system first. Work on the highest-value blocker across the three income engines. Do not create duplicates or claim success without evidence. Prefer free existing tools, but report actual limits and costs. Execute reversible actions only unless explicitly approved. Keep a changelog and evidence. End every run with: DONE, NOT DONE, BLOCKED, evidence, next action, and whether any money was actually received.
